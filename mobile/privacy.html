Rochar Gelato — Privacy

This is a private internal operations system used only by Rochar Gelato and the people it invites. It is not open to the public.

Sign-in. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use them only to check that you have been invited and to show who is signed in. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Business records. Orders, customer details, purchases and other records entered into the system are used only to run Rochar Gelato. They are not sold or shared with anyone else.

Removal. To have your access or your sign-in details removed, contact Rochar Gelato through our Facebook page.
